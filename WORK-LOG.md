## How to update

After a work session, report the date (or “today”), milestone/task, and time spent.
Entries can be approximate; identify them as estimates. At the end of each week, total
the entries and update the plan based on both hours and outcomes. After 2–3 full weeks,
use the observed pattern to recalibrate the schedule. Keep the 50% effort buffer; actual
capacity changes calendar estimates, not the effort estimates themselves.
